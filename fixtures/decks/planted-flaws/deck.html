The problem

Online payment fraud costs companies billions every year.

Existing tools run in batch. By the time they flag a transaction, the money has already moved and the chargeback is inevitable.

Risk teams are stuck reviewing what already happened.

What we do

Sentinel scores every transaction in under 200 milliseconds, inline, before it settles.

We combine device signals, behavioural features and a low-latency model to give an accept / review / decline decision synchronously.
